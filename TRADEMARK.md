## The basic idea

If someone sees the name **Idiot Box** next to a download, an app-store page or a screenshot, they should be looking at the real thing, made by us. Anything else must clearly say it is unofficial.

## Always allowed (no permission needed)

1. **Talking about the software.** You can use the name "Idiot Box" to describe it truthfully in articles, reviews, tutorials, videos, and in a fork's README ("a fork of Idiot Box", with a link to the official repository).
2. **Unmodified copies.** If you share the software exactly as we released it, keep the names, logo, copyright and license notices exactly as they are, and provide the source as the GPL requires. You may not present such a copy as your own product or as an official channel of ours.
3. **Contributing back.** Pull requests, issues, discussions and forks made to contribute to the official project may use the Marks for that purpose.
4. **The plain words, used honestly.** Naming something else "idiot box" is fine — see *What this file does not claim*.

## Modified versions and forks

If you change the software and share your version publicly:

1. **Rebrand it fully.** Remove "Idiot Box" from the app name, window title, About screen, icon, installer name, repository name, domain and any store listing. Use your own name and identity — this is the rule that stops our exact app from being re-released as somebody else's product.
2. **Say what it is, up front.** Where you do mention the origin (README, docs, credits), make it unmistakable: *"MyEditor, based on Idiot Box by The Wonderland Studio (unofficial fork)"* with a link to the official repository.
3. **Give credit.** Keep all existing copyright and license notices intact, and keep a visible credit line naming Idiot Box and The Wonderland Studio. Do not remove or hide attribution.
4. **Do not present it as official.** Nothing in your fork (name, icon, screenshots, description, domain, support channels) may suggest it is endorsed by, affiliated with, or the same product as the official Idiot Box.

## Never allowed without written permission

1. **Releasing our app as your own.** Taking Idiot Box essentially as-is — modified or just re-skinned — and publishing it as your own product in an app store, on a download site, or anywhere people would think you made it.
2. **Passing a build off as official.** Offering any build of this IDE (including an unmodified one) under the Idiot Box name as *the* official download, or implying you are the Idiot Box team.
3. **Confusing identities.** Putting "Idiot Box", "The Wonderland Studio", our logo, or confusingly similar names, icons or artwork on other software, websites, domains or merchandise.
4. **Removing or hiding credit.** Stripping copyright notices, license files, attribution lines or About information to make the work look like it has no origin.
5. **Implying endorsement.** Stating or suggesting that we sponsor, approve or are connected with your fork or your channel, unless we have said so in writing.
6. **Impersonating our channels.** Repositories, domains, social accounts or download pages that imitate the official ones above.
